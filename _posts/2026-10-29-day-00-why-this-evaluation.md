Over the next 30 days I am measuring one question: **does BugBunny's automated
scanning produce bounties that beat what I spend on audit credits?**

### What I will track

| Metric | How it is measured |
|---|---|
| Audit cost | credits consumed per target |
| Bounty return | confirmed payouts attributable to a BugBunny finding |
| Signal-to-noise | raw alerts vs. duplicates / non-issues |
| Proof quality | findings that survived triage with a validated PoC |

### Ground rules

- Every target is one I am authorised to test.
- No target data is copied, altered or published.
- Weekly roll-ups plus a final 30-day summary.

First real entry lands on 01 Nov.
